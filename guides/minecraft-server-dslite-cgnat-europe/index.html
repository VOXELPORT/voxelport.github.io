Minecraft Server Behind DS-Lite or CGNAT: Host It Anyway

Behind DS-Lite or CGNAT with no public IPv4? You can still host a Minecraft server: VoxelPort connects out to a relay, so nothing needs opening on your router.

You open port 25565 on your router, and your friends still cannot join. In many cases the port forwarding is fine. The problem is that your internet provider never gives your router a public IPv4 address of its own. This is the case on DS-Lite and CGNAT connections, which are used by several cable, fibre and mobile providers in Europe.

How to check which one you have

  1. Open your router's status page and look at the WAN IPv4 address.
  2. Open a "what is my IP" site on a device in your network and compare. If the two IPv4 addresses differ, or the router's address starts with 100.64 to 100.127, 10. or 192.168., you are behind CGNAT.
  3. If your router shows no IPv4 address at all but an IPv6 one, or its status page says DS-Lite, you are on DS-Lite.

Questions

What is DS-Lite?

Dual-Stack Lite gives your connection an IPv6 address and a shared IPv4 address that is used by many customers at once. Incoming IPv4 connections cannot reach your router, so port forwarding for IPv4 does not work.

What is CGNAT?

Carrier-grade NAT means your provider shares one public IPv4 address between many customers. Like DS-Lite, it blocks incoming IPv4 connections. It is also very common on mobile internet.

Can I just use IPv6 instead?

Only if every player also has IPv6, and many do not. A relay works for players on both IPv4 and IPv6, because they connect to the relay, not to your home connection.
