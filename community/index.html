The VoxelPort Discord

The VoxelPort Discord is where people ask for help, show their servers and hear about every release first. You do not need it to use VoxelPort.

  • Get help with setup, crashes and modpacks
  • Release announcements with download links
  • Show your server and find people to play with
  • Suggest features and report bugs

What is inside

HELP & SETUP

Stuck on a crash, a port or a modpack? Export an error report from the desktop app and attach it to a ticket in #open-a-ticket. The bot answers first and reads logs and screenshots. Press “I need a person for this” to stop AI replies and request our team.

RELEASES FIRST

Every new desktop and Android release is announced in #announcements with the download links. Press the button in the welcome channel if you want a ping.

SHOW YOUR SERVER

Post your address and your builds in #showcase, and find people to play on it.

SUGGEST & REPORT

Something broken, or a feature you want? Say so in #suggestions. This is where the next update comes from.

PICK YOUR ROLES

Java or Bedrock, phone host or PC host, modder: choose what fits in #roles so people know who to ask.

RELAY STATUS

Type /status in the server to see whether VoxelPort is working right now, and how many servers and players are online.
