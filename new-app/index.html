The new VoxelPort

VoxelPort has moved to a new app. It does everything the old one does, is about 3 MB instead of over 100 MB, and is the only one that gets new features. Moving takes about a minute and keeps your servers, worlds, backups and address.

  • Update your current app to 1.18.0 or newer, then press Update to the new VoxelPort
  • No button? Download the new app and it offers to bring your data over
  • Microsoft Store users: download the new app, bring your data over, remove the Store version
  • Your server folders and worlds are never touched

Move in one minute

  1. Update your current VoxelPort to 1.18.0 or newer (Restart & Update).
  2. Press Update to the new VoxelPort on the banner. It installs the new app, brings over your servers, backups, VoxelPort ID, address and settings, then removes the old app.
  3. Sign in with Discord once in the new app.

Other ways

No button? Download the new app and it offers to bring your data over. Microsoft Store users: the Store cannot install another app, so download it, bring your data over and remove the Store version. Windows may say it protected your PC: click More info, then Run anyway. With Windows 11 Smart App Control on, the installer is blocked; you can switch it off (Windows cannot turn it back on without reinstalling), or keep using your current VoxelPort, which keeps working.
