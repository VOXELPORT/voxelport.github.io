VoxelPort features

What the VoxelPort app does for the host, and why nobody who joins has to install anything: create a new server or bring your own, get a public address in seconds, and keep your router shut.

Features

INSTANT RELAY

Your server dials out to the relay and gets a public address in seconds. No DNS, no static IP, no router.

VANILLA CLIENTS

Friends join with plain Minecraft. No client mods, no launchers, no patches — ever.

ROUTER STAYS SHUT

Nothing to open on your side. Players connect to the relay, never to your home IP.

FRIENDS NEED NOTHING

Friends join with plain Minecraft: no account, no login, no mods. Hosts join our Discord server once to use the app, and a private device key is created on first run. Free, with no card.

DIRECT ROUTING

Hosts connect straight to the relay for ~10–20 ms pings, and fall back to Cloudflare automatically if a network blocks it.

FREE & OPEN

MIT-licensed app and relay. Read the code, fork it, or run your own relay.

YOUR ADDRESS

Every server gets a free address like play.voxelport.in:26137. Supporters can claim yourname.voxelport.in, with no port number.

BEDROCK TOO

One switch installs Geyser + Floodgate, so phone, console and PC players share one world.

CRASH HELP

If your server stops, VoxelPort says why in plain words — and offers the fix. Plus live CPU, RAM and TPS.
